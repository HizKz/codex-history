# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact.
- Respect privacy. Never expose conversation content, credentials, usernames,
  home-directory paths, or other personal information.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and community standards.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate privacy violations, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other
contributions that violate this policy. Depending on severity and recurrence,
responses may include a private warning, a temporary restriction, or a
permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private security advisory form](https://github.com/HizKz/codex-history/security/advisories/new).
Do not open a public issue when a report contains sensitive information.
Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible
and will disclose conflicts of interest.

This policy is based on the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
